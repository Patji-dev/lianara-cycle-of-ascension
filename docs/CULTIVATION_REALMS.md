# Cultivation realm catalogue

40 major realms, 190 minor stages, plus Mortal as the starting state. The full list is active in the playable game, from mortal cultivation to worlds, stars, universes and transcendence. Initial thresholds and realm effects live separately in the tuning configuration.

The existing first three major realms retain their names and stages. Later stage counts were randomly selected once within 3–7 and are now fixed in this catalogue; they do not reroll on reincarnation. Naming schemes vary between numerals, ranks and transformations specific to each realm.

The structured source is [cultivation-realms.json](../content/cultivation-realms.json). Tuning is in [cultivation-tuning.json](../content/cultivation-tuning.json); see [balance testing](BALANCE_TESTING.md).
